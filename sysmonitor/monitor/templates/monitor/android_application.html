What the app does

  • Loads the existing SysMonitor web portal and its normal login and role permissions.
  • Probes the local service and falls back to the configured external service.
  • Swipe down to refresh; loading progress, network indicator, retry prompt and log viewer.
  • Portrait-oriented interface with a splash screen.

Responsible use

The app displays the same server-side information permitted to your account. Do not share screenshots, credentials or operational data outside authorized recipients.

Install only the trusted official APK, verify its publisher before installing, and avoid entering credentials on an untrusted network. Keep your device and WebView updated.
