⏱️ Alarm Notification Cooldown
Controls how often a continuing temperature/humidity alarm can send another notification. Default is 30 minutes.
min
Example: 30 means another reminder can be sent only after 30 minutes if the alarm is still active.
Example: If the temperature crosses the configured high limit at 10:00, an alarm may be sent at 10:00. With a 30-minute cooldown, the same continuing condition will not send another notification before 10:30. If the temperature returns to normal and later crosses the limit again, that is treated as a new alarm condition.
